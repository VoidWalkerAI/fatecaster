============================================================

FateCaster

Cast a d20, apply a modifier, compare against a target number,

🪨 BLOCK 3 — CORE ROLL INVARIANTS
============================================================

1. A cast produces one randomized d20 result.

2. The result shown must be the result actually produced.

3. A cast result must never be silently changed after generation.

4. Modifiers must be applied consistently.

5. The modifier-adjusted final value must always be calculated,
   including on natural 1 and natural 20 rolls.

6. Target-number comparison must be deterministic once inputs are known.

7. Natural 20 always resolves as CRITICAL SUCCESS.

8. Natural 1 always resolves as CRITICAL FAILURE.

9. Natural overrides change the outcome tier, not the recorded roll math.

10. Future Broken Frontier invocation must use the same resolution rules.

11. Core rolling must work without network access.

12. Roll history must represent actual casts.

============================================================

Choose modifier
    ↓
Choose target number
    ↓
Cast fate
    ↓
Generate d20 result
    ↓
Calculate final value:

    ↓

Natural 1?
    → CRITICAL FAILURE
    ↓ otherwise
Compare final value to target number
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

🎮 BLOCK 6 — MODIFIERS
============================================================

DECISION FC-002 — MODIFIER RANGE

FateCaster v1 allows modifiers from:

-10 through +10

FORMULA:

raw d20 result + modifier = final roll value

Examples:

13 + 2 = 15
13 - 3 = 10

The final roll value is calculated on every cast,
including natural 1 and natural 20 casts.


============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 7 — TARGET NUMBERS
============================================================

DECISION FC-003 — TARGET NUMBER

FateCaster v1 uses:

TN 1 through 30

RULE:

When no natural-roll override applies:

final roll value >= TN = SUCCESS
final roll value <  TN = FAILURE

PHRASE:

Meet or beat.


DECISION FC-009 — EXTREME TARGET NUMBERS

The full TN 1 through 30 range is intentional.

Upper target numbers represent extreme difficulty.

Not every TN is expected to be readily attainable under every modifier.

This is not an error condition and does not change the natural-roll
override rules.


============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 8 — DEGREE OF SUCCESS
============================================================

DECISION FC-004 — DEGREE OF SUCCESS

When no natural-roll override applies, FateCaster v1 uses
four outcome levels based on final result relative to TN:

CRITICAL SUCCESS
    final result is 10 or more above TN

SUCCESS
    final result meets or exceeds TN
    but is less than 10 above TN

FAILURE
    final result is below TN
    but misses by less than 10

CRITICAL FAILURE
    final result is 10 or more below TN


============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 9 — NATURAL ROLL OVERRIDES
============================================================

DECISION FC-005 — NATURAL ROLL OVERRIDES

Natural 20 = ALWAYS CRITICAL SUCCESS

Natural 1 = ALWAYS CRITICAL FAILURE

RESOLUTION PRECEDENCE:

1. Generate the raw d20.
2. Calculate the final value using the modifier.
3. If raw d20 is 20, outcome = CRITICAL SUCCESS.
4. If raw d20 is 1, outcome = CRITICAL FAILURE.
5. Otherwise compare the final value to TN.
6. Determine degree of success from distance to TN.

Natural overrides take precedence over modifier/TN mathematics
for the OUTCOME TIER.


DECISION FC-007 — NATURAL OVERRIDE CALCULATION / DISPLAY CONTRACT

A natural 1 or natural 20 does NOT erase the modifier calculation.

Every cast preserves:

- raw d20 result
- modifier
- calculated final value
- target number
- final outcome tier
- whether a natural override occurred

Example:

raw d20: 20
modifier: -5
final value: 15
TN: 20
override: NATURAL 20
outcome: CRITICAL SUCCESS

The exact visual arrangement is still a UI decision,
but the app must not hide or falsify either the roll math
or the override outcome.

REFERENCE TEST VECTORS:

Raw 10 | Mod +0  | Final 10 | TN 10 → SUCCESS
Raw 19 | Mod +1  | Final 20 | TN 10 → CRITICAL SUCCESS
Raw 10 | Mod -1  | Final 9  | TN 10 → FAILURE
Raw 10 | Mod -10 | Final 0  | TN 10 → CRITICAL FAILURE
Raw 20 | Mod -5  | Final 15 | TN 20 → CRITICAL SUCCESS
                                  NATURAL 20 OVERRIDE
Raw 1  | Mod +10 | Final 11 | TN 5  → CRITICAL FAILURE
                                  NATURAL 1 OVERRIDE

DECISION FC-015 — CAST SCREEN CONTRACT

PRIMARY LAYOUT:

FateCaster uses a single-column CAST screen with:

- FateCaster identity/header
- large faceted d20 / raw-result area
- Modifier stepper
- Target TN stepper
- large full-width CAST FATE action
- completed-result area
- persistent CAST / HISTORY bottom navigation

The main content may scroll on short screens.

FateCaster does not force portrait orientation.


INPUT CONTROLS:

Modifier:
- tap stepper only
- range -10 through +10
- one tap changes value by one
- no slider
- no keyboard entry
- no wraparound

Target TN:
- tap stepper only
- range 1 through 30
- one tap changes value by one
- no slider
- no keyboard entry
- no wraparound

At a range boundary, the unavailable direction is disabled.


DEFAULT CAST STATE:

Fresh launch:

Modifier:
+0

Target TN:
10

Result:
None

The d20 area shows a neutral placeholder.
No fabricated roll is displayed.


D20 PRESENTATION:

The dominant visual element is a recognizable faceted d20.

The number shown inside the d20 is always the RAW d20 result.

The modifier-adjusted final value is shown separately.

During casting, the d20 may tumble / rotate / scale as presentation.

Do not display fake intermediate roll numbers.


CAST ACTION:

The user sets Modifier and TN and taps CAST FATE once.

No confirmation or separate calculation action is required.

A cast is atomic.

At CAST FATE tap:

- capture Modifier
- capture TN
- begin CASTING state
- freeze cast inputs
- disable CAST FATE
- temporarily disable navigation
- generate exactly one authoritative raw d20 result
- resolve that result using FC-001 through FC-009
- write exactly one history record
- display the completed result
- re-enable controls


CAST SCREEN STATES:

1. READY

- neutral d20 placeholder
- Modifier/TN controls active
- CAST FATE active
- no fabricated result

2. CASTING

- captured inputs cannot change
- "Awaiting your Fate…" is displayed
- d20 presentation animation runs
- one authoritative raw d20 is generated
- no fake intermediate rolls

3. RESULT

- large d20 shows raw roll
- "Your Fate has been cast: [FINAL]"
- explicit outcome tier is displayed
- NATURAL 1 / NATURAL 20 is displayed when applicable
- raw / modifier / final math is displayed
- TN used for that cast is displayed
- exactly one history record exists for the cast


RESULT HIERARCHY:

The interface communicates:

1. what Fate rolled
2. what the modifier changed it to
3. what outcome FateCaster resolved

Example:

[ LARGE D20 ]
17

Your Fate has been cast: 19

SUCCESS

Raw 17 +2 = 19
TN 15

Natural override example:

[ LARGE D20 ]
20

Your Fate has been cast: 15

CRITICAL SUCCESS
NATURAL 20

Raw 20 -5 = 15
TN 20


OUTCOME ACCESSIBILITY:

Outcome meaning must always be written explicitly:

- CRITICAL SUCCESS
- SUCCESS
- FAILURE
- CRITICAL FAILURE

Color, weight, and animation may reinforce meaning,
but color must never be the only signal.


REPEATED CAST BEHAVIOR:

Modifier and TN remain where the user set them during the current app session.

The previous completed result remains visible until another cast begins.

Changing Modifier or TN prepares the NEXT cast.

Changing those controls must not alter or erase the previous completed result.

When another cast begins, the completed result transitions to:

"Awaiting your Fate…"


SESSION / RESTART BEHAVIOR:

During the current app session:

- Modifier persists
- TN persists
- last completed result persists

On a fresh app launch:

- Modifier resets to +0
- TN resets to 10
- CAST result returns to no-result state

Persistent HISTORY is not erased by app restart.


NAVIGATION:

CAST and HISTORY are persistent bottom-navigation destinations.

No hamburger menu is required.

No swipe gesture is required.

Both primary screens remain one tap apart.


RULE:

The CAST UI must never redefine or duplicate authoritative
resolution mathematics.

FC-001 through FC-009 remain authoritative.

The screen presents and coordinates a cast;
the pure Kotlin roll resolver resolves it.

DECISION FC-016 — HISTORY SCREEN CONTRACT

PRIMARY LAYOUT:

FateCaster HISTORY uses one vertically scrolling list.

Ordering is fixed:

newest cast first
oldest retained cast last

Older casts continue downward through the list.

HISTORY uses the same persistent bottom navigation as CAST.

HISTORY is selected while viewing this screen.


HISTORY ITEM:

Each retained cast is displayed as one self-contained,
read-only history item.

Each item displays:

- final roll value
- explicit outcome tier
- NATURAL 1 / NATURAL 20 when applicable
- raw d20 result
- modifier
- calculated final value
- target number
- one cast timestamp containing date and time

Example:

19                         SUCCESS

Raw 17 +2 = 19
TN 15

Aug 16, 2026 · 10:00 AM


Natural override example:

15                CRITICAL SUCCESS

NATURAL 20

Raw 20 -5 = 15
TN 20

Aug 16, 2026 · 9:53 AM


TIMESTAMP:

Each cast has one timestamp.

The timestamp represents the date and time the cast occurred.

Use the device's normal local date/time presentation.

FateCaster v1 does not require a special proprietary,
military, or secondary timestamp format.


HISTORY ITEM INTERACTION:

History items are records, not controls.

For v1, history items are not:

- editable
- individually deletable
- tappable for a detail screen
- swipeable for actions
- reroll controls
- long-press menus

There is no separate history-detail screen.


RETENTION / LIST BEHAVIOR:

FateCaster displays all currently retained casts
in the same scrolling list.

Maximum retained history:

100 casts

No pagination.

No Load More action.

No search.

No filtering.

No sorting control.

No grouping by date.

Ordering is always newest first.

When cast 101 is created,
the oldest retained cast is removed according to FC-008
and the new cast becomes the first history item.


CLEAR HISTORY:

Clear History is a visible secondary action
in the HISTORY header.

Example:

HISTORY                         Clear History

Clear History must not erase records immediately.

Selecting Clear History presents one confirmation:

Clear history?

This will remove all saved casts.

[ Cancel ]     [ Clear ]

Cancel:

- changes nothing
- closes the confirmation

Clear:

- deletes all retained casts
- keeps the user on HISTORY
- immediately displays the empty-history state

When HISTORY is empty,
Clear History remains visible but is disabled.


EMPTY HISTORY:

When no casts are retained, HISTORY displays:

No casts yet.

Cast your fate to begin.

Do not display:

- fabricated example casts
- fake history records
- required tutorial cards

After Clear History succeeds,
the same empty state is displayed.

The user is not automatically redirected to CAST.


CURRENT-RECORD BEHAVIOR:

A completed cast is written to HISTORY immediately.

The next time HISTORY is viewed,
the completed cast is already present as the newest item.

HISTORY requires:

- no manual refresh
- no refresh button
- no pull-to-refresh
- no new-result banner

HISTORY always represents the current retained record.


RULE:

HISTORY is a simple local record of completed casts.

It must not silently evolve into a roll-management,
editing, search, or analytics system during FateCaster v1.
