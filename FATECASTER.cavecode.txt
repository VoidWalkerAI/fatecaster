🪨 BLOCK 1 — PRODUCT IDENTITY
============================================================

NAME:
FateCaster

PRODUCT TYPE:
Standalone Android application.

PRIMARY PURPOSE:
Cast a d20, apply a modifier, compare against a target number,
show the degree of outcome, and preserve recent roll history.

SECONDARY PURPOSE:
Provide a small, real application that can be taken through
the complete Android and Google Play Store release lifecycle.

BROKEN FRONTIER RELATIONSHIP:
FateCaster is related to Broken Frontier but must remain
usable as a standalone application.

Broken Frontier integration is future work.

FateCaster v1 must not require Broken Frontier to function.

CAVECODE ROLE:
FateCaster is CaveCode Reference Implementation 001.

🪨 BLOCK 3 — CORE ROLL INVARIANTS
============================================================

1. A cast produces one randomized d20 result.

2. The result shown must be the result actually produced.

3. A cast result must never be silently changed after generation.

4. Modifiers must be applied consistently.

5. The modifier-adjusted final value must always be calculated,
   including on natural 1 and natural 20 rolls.

6. Target-number comparison must be deterministic once inputs are known.

7. Natural 20 always resolves as CRITICAL SUCCESS.

8. Natural 1 always resolves as CRITICAL FAILURE.

9. Natural overrides change the outcome tier, not the recorded roll math.

10. Future Broken Frontier invocation must use the same resolution rules.

11. Core rolling must work without network access.

12. Roll history must represent actual casts.

============================================================

Choose modifier
    ↓
Choose target number
    ↓
Cast fate
    ↓
Generate d20 result
    ↓
Calculate final value:
raw d20 + modifier
    ↓
Natural 20?
    → CRITICAL SUCCESS
    ↓ otherwise
Natural 1?
    → CRITICAL FAILURE
    ↓ otherwise
Compare final value to target number
    ↓
Determine degree of outcome
    ↓
Display result
    ↓
Record cast in local history

============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 6 — MODIFIERS
============================================================

DECISION FC-002 — MODIFIER RANGE

FateCaster v1 allows modifiers from:

-10 through +10

FORMULA:

raw d20 result + modifier = final roll value

Examples:

13 + 2 = 15
13 - 3 = 10

The final roll value is calculated on every cast,
including natural 1 and natural 20 casts.


============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 7 — TARGET NUMBERS
============================================================

DECISION FC-003 — TARGET NUMBER

FateCaster v1 uses:

TN 1 through 30

RULE:

When no natural-roll override applies:

final roll value >= TN = SUCCESS
final roll value <  TN = FAILURE

PHRASE:

Meet or beat.


DECISION FC-009 — EXTREME TARGET NUMBERS

The full TN 1 through 30 range is intentional.

Upper target numbers represent extreme difficulty.

Not every TN is expected to be readily attainable under every modifier.

This is not an error condition and does not change the natural-roll
override rules.


============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 8 — DEGREE OF SUCCESS
============================================================

DECISION FC-004 — DEGREE OF SUCCESS

When no natural-roll override applies, FateCaster v1 uses
four outcome levels based on final result relative to TN:

CRITICAL SUCCESS
    final result is 10 or more above TN

SUCCESS
    final result meets or exceeds TN
    but is less than 10 above TN

FAILURE
    final result is below TN
    but misses by less than 10

CRITICAL FAILURE
    final result is 10 or more below TN


============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 9 — NATURAL ROLL OVERRIDES
============================================================

DECISION FC-005 — NATURAL ROLL OVERRIDES

Natural 20 = ALWAYS CRITICAL SUCCESS

Natural 1 = ALWAYS CRITICAL FAILURE

RESOLUTION PRECEDENCE:

1. Generate the raw d20.
2. Calculate the final value using the modifier.
3. If raw d20 is 20, outcome = CRITICAL SUCCESS.
4. If raw d20 is 1, outcome = CRITICAL FAILURE.
5. Otherwise compare the final value to TN.
6. Determine degree of success from distance to TN.

Natural overrides take precedence over modifier/TN mathematics
for the OUTCOME TIER.


DECISION FC-007 — NATURAL OVERRIDE CALCULATION / DISPLAY CONTRACT

A natural 1 or natural 20 does NOT erase the modifier calculation.

Every cast preserves:

- raw d20 result
- modifier
- calculated final value
- target number
- final outcome tier
- whether a natural override occurred

Example:

raw d20: 20
modifier: -5
final value: 15
TN: 20
override: NATURAL 20
outcome: CRITICAL SUCCESS

The exact visual arrangement is still a UI decision,
but the app must not hide or falsify either the roll math
or the override outcome.

REFERENCE TEST VECTORS:

Raw 10 | Mod +0  | Final 10 | TN 10 → SUCCESS
Raw 19 | Mod +1  | Final 20 | TN 10 → CRITICAL SUCCESS
Raw 10 | Mod -1  | Final 9  | TN 10 → FAILURE
Raw 10 | Mod -10 | Final 0  | TN 10 → CRITICAL FAILURE
Raw 20 | Mod -5  | Final 15 | TN 20 → CRITICAL SUCCESS
                                  NATURAL 20 OVERRIDE
Raw 1  | Mod +10 | Final 11 | TN 5  → CRITICAL FAILURE
                                  NATURAL 1 OVERRIDE

🎮 BLOCK 12 — LOCAL ROLL HISTORY
============================================================

FateCaster v1 includes local roll history.

MINIMUM RECORD:

- timestamp
- raw d20 result
- modifier
- final roll value
- target number
- degree-of-success result
- whether a natural 1 or natural 20 override occurred

STORAGE PRINCIPLE:

Local-first.

No account or remote service is required to preserve history.


DECISION FC-008 — HISTORY RETENTION

FateCaster v1 retains the most recent:

100 casts

When a new cast would exceed the limit,
the oldest retained cast is removed.

The HISTORY screen includes a Clear History action.

Clear History must require an intentional user action.

This does not create a third primary screen.

============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 14 — V1 SCREEN MODEL
============================================================

DECISION FC-006 — PRIMARY SCREENS

FateCaster v1 uses two primary screens:

1. CAST
2. HISTORY


CAST SCREEN:

The main operational screen.

Must contain:

- d20 identity / presentation
- modifier control
- target number control
- CAST FATE action
- cast animation or waiting state
- raw/final result information
- degree-of-success display
- visible natural 1 / natural 20 status when applicable


HISTORY SCREEN:

Displays previous casts.

Each visible history item should provide enough information
to understand the roll without opening a separate detail screen.

The HISTORY screen also provides the Clear History action
defined in FC-008.


V1 DOES NOT REQUIRE:

- account screen
- profile screen
- tutorial flow
- dedicated settings screen

If small settings such as sound or haptics are implemented,
they may be exposed from the CAST screen without creating
a third primary screen.

Android foundation:

Language:

Jetpack Compose

Single Activity

Architecture:
Small layered architecture.

UI layer:
- CAST screen
- HISTORY screen

- pure Kotlin roll resolver

Data layer:

DECISION FC-011 — APPLICATION ID

Application ID:
ai.voidwalker.fatecaster

Android namespace:
ai.voidwalker.fatecaster

RULE:
Keep the Android namespace and application ID identical
unless a future technical requirement gives a concrete reason
to separate them.

The application ID is release-critical identity and must not be
casually changed after publication.

DECISION FC-012 — MINIMUM SDK

Minimum SDK:
API 23

Android version:
Android 6.0 Marshmallow

WHY:

FateCaster is a new application using the current AndroidX
and Jetpack Compose ecosystem.

API 23 provides very broad device compatibility without requiring
FateCaster to carry unnecessary compatibility work for older devices.

RULE:

Do not lower the minimum SDK merely to support obsolete Android
versions unless a real user or deployment requirement justifies it.

DECISION FC-013 — TARGET SDK

Target SDK:
API 36

Android version:
Android 16

WHY:

FateCaster is a new Google Play application.

FateCaster is being built immediately before Google Play's
API 36 target requirement takes effect for new phone/tablet apps
and updates.

Targeting API 36 from the beginning avoids creating an obsolete
release baseline that would need to be changed almost immediately.

RULE:

FateCaster should target the current Google Play-required API
level at release time.

Future target SDK changes are release-maintenance work and should
be recorded in CaveCode before implementation.

DECISION FC-014 — INITIAL VERSION

versionCode:
1

versionName:
0.1.0

WHY:

versionCode 1 is FateCaster's first Android build identity.

versionName 0.1.0 identifies FateCaster as an early
pre-release implementation build.

The first production-ready Google Play release may advance
versionName to 1.0.0 when the v1 product contract is actually satisfied.

RULE:

versionCode must increase for every subsequent build uploaded
to Google Play.

Never reuse a versionCode that has already been uploaded.

versionName communicates the meaningful product release version
and does not need to numerically match versionCode.
