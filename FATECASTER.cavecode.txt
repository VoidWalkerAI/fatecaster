🪨 BLOCK 3 — CORE ROLL INVARIANTS
============================================================

1. A cast produces one randomized d20 result.
2. The result shown must be the result actually produced.
3. A cast result must never be silently changed after generation.
4. Modifiers must be applied consistently.
5. The modifier-adjusted final value must always be calculated,
   including on natural 1 and natural 20 rolls.
6. Target-number comparison must be deterministic once inputs are known.
7. Natural 20 always resolves as CRITICAL SUCCESS.
8. Natural 1 always resolves as CRITICAL FAILURE.
9. Natural overrides change the outcome tier, not the recorded roll math.
10. Future Broken Frontier invocation must use the same resolution rules.
11. Core rolling must work without network access.
12. Roll history must represent actual casts.

🎮 BLOCK 4 — PRIMARY ROLL FLOW
============================================================

Choose modifier
    ↓
Choose target number
    ↓
Cast fate
    ↓
Generate d20 result
    ↓
Calculate final value:
raw d20 + modifier
    ↓
Natural 20?
    → CRITICAL SUCCESS
    ↓ otherwise
Natural 1?
    → CRITICAL FAILURE
    ↓ otherwise
Compare final value to target number
    ↓
Determine degree of outcome
    ↓
Display result
    ↓
Record cast in local history

THIS FLOW IS NOW IMPLEMENTED AND PHYSICALLY PROVEN THROUGH HISTORY WRITE.


============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 5 — SETTLED ROLL DECISIONS
============================================================

FC-001 — V1 DICE

d20 only.

FC-002 — MODIFIER RANGE

-10 through +10.

Formula:
raw d20 + modifier = final value

FC-003 — TARGET NUMBER

TN 1 through 30.
Meet or beat succeeds.

FC-004 — DEGREE OF SUCCESS

When no natural override applies:

CRITICAL SUCCESS
    final result is 10 or more above TN

SUCCESS
    final result meets or exceeds TN
    but is less than 10 above TN

FAILURE
    final result is below TN
    but misses by less than 10

CRITICAL FAILURE
    final result is 10 or more below TN

FC-005 — NATURAL OVERRIDES

Natural 20 = ALWAYS CRITICAL SUCCESS
Natural 1 = ALWAYS CRITICAL FAILURE

Natural overrides affect the outcome tier only.
The modifier-adjusted final value is still calculated and preserved.

FC-007 — NATURAL OVERRIDE DISPLAY CONTRACT

Every cast preserves:

- raw d20 result
- modifier
- calculated final value
- target number
- final outcome tier
- whether a natural override occurred

Reference vectors:

Raw 10 | Mod +0  | Final 10 | TN 10 → SUCCESS
Raw 19 | Mod +1  | Final 20 | TN 10 → CRITICAL SUCCESS
Raw 10 | Mod -1  | Final 9  | TN 10 → FAILURE
Raw 10 | Mod -10 | Final 0  | TN 10 → CRITICAL FAILURE
Raw 20 | Mod -5  | Final 15 | TN 20 → CRITICAL SUCCESS / NATURAL 20
Raw 1  | Mod +10 | Final 11 | TN 5  → CRITICAL FAILURE / NATURAL 1

FC-009 — EXTREME TARGET NUMBERS

The full TN 1 through 30 range is intentional.
Upper TNs represent extreme difficulty and need not be readily attainable
under every modifier.

🎮 BLOCK 7 — LOCAL HISTORY CONTRACT
============================================================

FC-008 — HISTORY RETENTION

FateCaster v1 retains the most recent 100 casts.
When cast 101 is created, the oldest retained cast is removed.

Minimum stored record:

- timestamp
- raw d20 result
- modifier
- final roll value
- target number
- degree-of-success result
- natural override state

Storage principle:
Local-first.

No account or remote service is required.

Clear History is part of the settled v1 contract.
It requires intentional user action and confirmation.

IMPORTANT CURRENT TRUTH:

The storage layer already supports clearing history.
The current HISTORY user interface does NOT yet expose the required
Clear History action or confirmation dialog.

🎮 BLOCK 9 — SCREEN MODEL
============================================================

FC-006 — PRIMARY SCREENS

1. CAST
2. HISTORY

No third primary screen is required for v1.
CAST and HISTORY are one tap apart through persistent bottom navigation.


FC-015 — CAST SCREEN CONTRACT

The CAST screen uses a single-column layout containing:

- FateCaster identity/header
- large faceted d20 / raw-result area
- Modifier stepper
- Target TN stepper
- large CAST FATE action
- completed-result area
- CAST / HISTORY bottom navigation

Modifier:
- tap stepper
- -10 through +10
- one point per tap
- no slider
- no keyboard entry
- no wraparound

Target TN:
- tap stepper
- 1 through 30
- one point per tap
- no slider
- no keyboard entry
- no wraparound

Fresh launch:
- Modifier +0
- TN 10
- no fabricated result
- neutral d20 placeholder

CAST STATES:

READY
- controls active
- no fabricated result

CASTING
- captured inputs frozen
- CAST FATE disabled
- navigation temporarily disabled
- "Awaiting your Fate…" displayed
- d20 presentation animation runs
- exactly one authoritative raw d20 is generated

RESULT
- d20 shows raw roll
- final value shown separately
- explicit outcome tier displayed
- natural override shown when applicable
- raw/modifier/final math displayed
- TN displayed
- exactly one history record written

SESSION BEHAVIOR:

During the current app session:
- Modifier persists
- TN persists
- last completed result persists

Fresh app launch:
- Modifier resets to +0
- TN resets to 10
- CAST result returns to no-result state

Persistent HISTORY is not erased by an ordinary app restart.

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION TRUTH:

FC-015 is implemented.
The CAST screen has been exercised successfully on physical Android hardware.


FC-016 — HISTORY SCREEN CONTRACT

HISTORY is one vertically scrolling, newest-first list.
Maximum retained history: 100 casts.

Each item is read-only and should communicate:

- final roll value
- explicit outcome tier
- NATURAL 1 / NATURAL 20 when applicable
- raw d20 result
- modifier
- calculated final value
- target number
- local date/time timestamp

History items are not:

- editable
- individually deletable
- detail-screen links
- swipe actions
- reroll controls
- long-press menus

No search.
No filters.
No alternate sorting.
No pagination.
No manual refresh.

CLEAR HISTORY CONTRACT:

Visible secondary HISTORY action.
Requires confirmation:

Clear history?
This will remove all saved casts.

[ Cancel ]     [ Clear ]

Clear keeps the user on HISTORY and immediately shows the empty state.
When empty, Clear History remains visible but disabled.

EMPTY STATE CONTRACT:

No casts yet.
Cast your fate to begin.

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION TRUTH:

Implemented and physically proven:

- HISTORY destination opens
- CAST destination returns to CAST
- newest-first stored records display
- outcome tier displays
- raw/modifier/final arithmetic displays
- TN displays
- local timestamp displays
- natural override label is implemented when applicable
- empty-history state displays "No casts yet."
- completed casts appear when HISTORY is opened

Still incomplete against FC-016:

- Clear History UI action is missing
- Clear History confirmation dialog is missing
- empty-state second line "Cast your fate to begin." is missing
- final-value presentation may still be refined to match the exact FC-016 hierarchy

Therefore FC-016 is NOT yet fully implemented even though the HISTORY screen
is already functional and physically proven in its present form.
